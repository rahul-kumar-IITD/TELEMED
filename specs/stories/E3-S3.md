# E3-S3: Patient or doctor can cancel an appointment

- Epic: E3 (Appointment Booking and Lifecycle)
- Layer: API
- Group: G
- Estimate: M
- Depends on: E3-S2
- Brief criteria covered: AC-06

## Description

Patients may cancel until 60 minutes before start; the slot is released and the refund stub is called once. Doctors may cancel BOOKED appointments at any time; the slot becomes BLOCKED. Every cancellation appends one event recording the actor role.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A patient cancelling exactly 60:00 before start receives 200, the appointment becomes CANCELLED, the slot returns to AVAILABLE, one CANCELLED event is appended and the refund stub is called exactly once.
2. A patient cancelling at 59:59 before start, or after the start time, receives 409 and nothing changes and the refund stub is not called.
3. A patient cancelling another patient's appointment receives 404.
4. A doctor cancelling a BOOKED appointment 10 minutes before start receives 200, the slot becomes BLOCKED, the event records actor role DOCTOR and the refund stub is called exactly once.
5. Cancelling a CHECKED_IN, IN_PROGRESS, COMPLETED, CANCELLED or NO_SHOW appointment returns 409 InvalidAppointmentStateException and appends no event.
