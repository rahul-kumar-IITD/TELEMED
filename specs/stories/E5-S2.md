# E5-S2: Patient UI for doctor search, slot picking and booking

- Epic: E5 (Frontend Experience)
- Layer: UI
- Group: G
- Estimate: L
- Depends on: E5-S1, E2-S4, E3-S2
- Brief criteria covered: AC-02, AC-03, AC-04

## Description

Patient-facing screens, designed 375px-first: doctor search with filters and sort, doctor detail with a 14-day slot picker, and booking confirmation. Times show in the browser-local zone with an abbreviation.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The search page offers specialty, language, date-range filters and a sort control, lists results with the fee such as "500.00", and shows an empty state when nothing matches.
2. The doctor detail page shows open slots for the next 14 days grouped by date with times in browser-local zone and abbreviation such as '10:30 IST'.
3. Selecting a slot and confirming shows a booking confirmation with doctor, local time and fee.
4. A 409 on booking shows an inline message and refreshes the slot list so the taken slot disappears.
5. At 375px the patient screens have no horizontal scroll and every interactive control is at least 44px by 44px.
